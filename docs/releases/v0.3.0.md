## What's new in 0.3.0

**Signed updates.** Releases are signed with the project's release key, and commonplace installs one only after checking the signature and every byte of the installer against it. Copies installed from source move to exactly the commit a signed release names.

**Different versions working together.** Computers say which version of commonplace they run. If two can't sync a file, both are told who needs to update, and nothing is lost in the meantime. A file changed by a newer version opens read-only in an older one.

**A "can comment" role.** Invite people to comment without letting them edit. Every computer checks that a commenter's changes touch the comments and nothing else.

**Always-on relays keep uploaded files.** Uploaded files now download from a relay while whoever uploaded them is away. The relay stores them encrypted, under names it can't link to their contents.

**A network that keeps itself going.** commonplace remembers the computers it meets on the network and starts from them next time, so the public bootstrap addresses are only needed once. A group can list its own bootstrap node under Settings → Network.

**Trash and stars.** Removing a file moves it to the trash, with Undo. It keeps syncing there until you empty the trash, or until it empties itself after 30 days (a setting). Star the files you use most and find them under Starred.

**Cut off a lost computer.** Let your recovery key protect your account: the key that is you gets locked away, and each computer gets a key of its own. Removing a lost or stolen computer then cuts it off for real: nobody accepts what it signs, and your files move to new keys it never gets. The recovery key brings you back on a new computer, or takes your account back if every computer was removed.

**People who can share.** "Can edit and share" lets someone invite, let in and remove people (everyone but you and others who can share), so files don't wait on your computer.

**This computer as a relay.** Settings → Always-on relay can now keep files for others right inside commonplace, with its own address and size limit. While it's on, commonplace keeps running in the tray when its window closes.

**Light, dark or system theme**, under Settings → Appearance and writing.

**Keyboard shortcuts.** Press Ctrl+/ (⌘/ on a Mac) to see all of them.

**Smaller things.** The Share dialog is tidier, toasts and menus show above open dialogs, stars toggle with one click, and first run offers bringing your Drive from another computer. Every build is now checked against the last two releases.

Also: the spelling setting now saves as soon as you change it, and the names of people who can only view or comment show on their comments.

**Before you update.** Computers on 0.3.0 still sync with ones on 0.2.0. One exception: 0.2.0 doesn't know the comment role, so once you invite someone to comment on a file, people with that file still on 0.2.0 stop following its access changes until they update. From 0.3.0 on, a role an older app doesn't know counts as view-only there instead. Two more need everyone in a file on 0.3.0: people who can share, and protection (once your account is protected, people on older versions can't check your computers' signatures).
